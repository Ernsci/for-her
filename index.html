a message written in the stars

Rei, Rei, I’m sorry.

There’s something I’ve been wanting to say to you — properly, this time.

chapter one

I owe you an honest apology

No fancy words. Just how I actually talk:

I’m sorry, Rei.
I’m dumb — dumb enough
to hurt the one person
I never, ever wanted to hurt.

My actions made it look like
I treat my girl friends the same as you.
But it’s wrong, and it’s not true.
They’re them. You’re you.
You were never the same to me.

And honestly?
We’ve never even dated,
so you never got to feel
the way I actually love someone.
It’s not that it doesn’t exist —
I’ve been saving all of it for you.

chapter two

Among a thousand stars

Sailors don’t steer by the whole sky. They find one star — and trust it with everything.

  • They get a “see you later.” You get a “I can’t wait to see you.”
  • They get jokes. You get the truth.
  • Their calls get answered. Your name alone makes my whole day.

every path in my sky leads back to you

There is exactly one of you, Rei. And I’m sorry I ever let you question it.

chapter three

I keep replaying this

our very first conversation — we laughed like the joke was hilarious, even though it really, truly wasn’t

That’s when I knew. You can’t fake a laugh like that. It’s still one of my favorite sounds in the world, and honestly? I’d sit through that terrible joke a hundred more times just to hear it again.

That laugh isn’t something I share with everyone. That’s yours.

chapter four

About our first date

Yes — our first. We haven’t had it yet, and I know shyness and busy college days have had their share in that. So let me make this promise properly, with zero pressure attached:

  • ✦Whenever you’re ready — next week or next semester — I’ll be ready too. There’s no deadline on us.
  • ✦When that day comes, I’ll plan everything down to the last detail. All you have to do is show up.
  • ✦Shy, quiet, nervous — it’s all okay. I didn’t fall for a version of you without the shy parts.

the last thing

So… Rei

Can you forgive me?

made under the same sky you’re looking at tonight — by someone who is very, very sorry, and even more in love with you
